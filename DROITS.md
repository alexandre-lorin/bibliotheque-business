# Collection and reuse

Added October 27: A complete official PDF, *Introduction to Business* from OpenStax, is archived and extracted with its CC BY notice 4.0 read in the first pages of this exact edition. This license should not be extrapolated to other editions or current versions of the site. Two other OpenStax downloads were refused and not bypassed. Local copies of Toubi documents remain for its local use.

The `books.py` module keeps the originals and rights declared for each import. Signed links and account keys are not recorded in the records. The Anna’s Archive option requires access provided by Toubi and declared rights; it was not validated with a real account. See `RAG.md`.

## Texts preserved in full

Markdown files for `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` and `blader/humanizer` are maintained from revision-specific archives. Their MIT license was checked at the root of the archive before import. The notices are in `licences/`; the original archives are kept in `archives/` and their fingerprints in the reports `data/collecte-*.json`.

Imported content is not executed or installed as active skills. The collector only reads ordinary Markdown files selected from archives, without extracting paths to disk. Any orders and instructions present remain documentary.

The MIT license authorizes the reuse of these files with retention of the notice. It does not guarantee any third party claims, results, trademarks or data that a guide may mention. The commercial examples in the corpus do not represent promised results for Toubi projects.

## Articles, conferences and other corpora

Other sites are referenced by URL and catalog metadata. Their articles, videos, transcripts and datasets are not vacuumed up in full. Titles from URLs are explicitly marked as inferred; data from public catalogs is used for retrieval purposes only.

The collector respects robot refusals and the deadlines indicated, limits the size of the responses and does not use any accounts or cookies. It does not bypass paywalls, browser challenges or blocking. Public content on a page does not imply general permission for redistribution, translation, or copying.

References to AdParaphrase and Anthropic Persuasion indicate non-commercial licenses. Amazon reviews are not presented as a free commercial corpus. For TED, consult the official policy before any use: no TED videos or transcripts have been collected. The Scientific Advertising notice is referenced; the book has not been downloaded.

## Sheets and skills of Toubi

The 24 French sheets and the skills `toubi-humanizer` and `toubi-bibliotheque` are original drafts of this delivery. They are not redistributing a translation of the Humanizer skill. The third-party repository is kept separately as a reference for comparison, with its license notice. The known communication preferences of Toubi were used for presetting, without claiming to constitute training of his personal voice.

The sheets provide working methods and hypotheses to test. They do not contain false testimonies or results presented as measured.

## What remains distinct

A searchable library does not constitute a validated training game. Future use for training, publication of a corpus or a commercial service would require checking the rights corresponding to each document and use. No deletion of source archives is automated.
